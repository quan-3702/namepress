NamePress – How to use

Print name badges and folded place cards from a simple list of names.

1. Choose badges or place cards2. Type the names3. Top and bottom lines4. Design, colour and lettering5. Print name badges6. Print and fold place cards7. Add your logo (Pro)8. Free version and Pro9. If something goes wrong

1. Choose badges or place cards

  • Name badges: printed on sheets of sticker labels that people wear. Choose the sheet that matches your pack, for example 8 badges of 2⅓ × 3⅜ in (Avery 5395 size).
  • Place cards: printed on plain card, cut out and folded in half to stand on a table. Small cards hold 4 per sheet; large table cards hold 2.

2. Type the names

  1. In One name on each line, type or paste your list. You get one badge or card for each line.
  2. To add a second line under a name, put a comma after the name: Michael Carter, Table 2 or Sophia Nguyen, Sales.
  3. Very long names are made a little smaller so they always fit.
You can copy a column of names from Excel or Word and paste it straight into the box.

3. Top and bottom lines

  • Line at the top: for example HELLO my name is, Welcome or the name of your club. With the Hello design, HELLO is printed in large letters.
  • Line at the bottom: for example the event name and date. Leave it empty if you do not need it.

4. Design, colour and lettering

Click one of the small pictures to choose a design and a coloured circle for the colour. Lettering for the name offers Modern, Classic and Handwriting, which looks lovely on wedding place cards.

5. Print name badges

  1. Click Print a test page and print it on plain paper.
  2. Hold the test page behind a sheet of badges against a window. The boxes should sit on the badges.
  3. If they are a little off, type a few millimetres in Move right or Move down and test again.
  4. Put the badge sheet in the printer and click 🖨 Print. Set Scale to 100%.
Some badges already used? Type the number of the first free badge in Start at badge no. Badges are counted left to right, top to bottom.

6. Print and fold place cards

  1. Click 🖨 Print and set Scale to 100%. Card stock gives the best result.
  2. Cut along the light dotted line around each card.
  3. Fold along the faint line in the middle so that the printing is on the outside.
  4. With Print the name on both sides of the fold ticked, guests on both sides of the table can read the name.

7. Add your logo (Pro)

Click Add a logo picture… and choose a picture file. It appears on every badge and card. Click Remove logo to take it away.

8. Free version and Pro

The free version never limits how often you print.

  • Free: 3 designs, up to 24 names at a time, all sheets and colours.
  • Pro (one-time purchase): all 6 designs, any number of names at a time, your own logo.

9. If something goes wrong

  • The colours are missing: turn on Background graphics in the print window.
  • The badges drift further off towards the bottom: set Scale to 100% or Actual size.
  • My names are gone on another PC: they are stored only on the PC where you typed them.
